# 9 Notion Templates That Actually Save Time

Most Notion templates are interior design: beautiful dashboards nobody opens twice. These nine are *systems* — each replaces a recurring decision or a scattered workflow. Build or copy them once; they pay rent weekly.

## 1. The Single Task Inbox

One database, five fields: task, project (relation), due date, energy (high/low), status. Every task in your life enters here. Each morning, filter to "today + high energy" and work top-down. Why it works: it kills the "which of my 4 lists matters?" tax.

## 2. Meeting Notes with Auto-Actions

Template button inside your notes DB that inserts: attendees, decisions (checkbox list), and action items pre-assigned with dates. Review the "open actions" view every Friday. This alone ends "wait, who was doing that?" threads.

## 3. Job Hunt Tracker

Applications DB with: company, role, stage (select), last contact, next action + date, and a linked "contacts" DB. The killer view: "stale" filter (no contact in 7 days) → your Monday follow-up list writes itself.

## 4. Reading List with Spaced Review

Save articles with a 1-line "why I saved this" (mandatory — it filters junk). A formula surfaces items unread for 30+ days for a keep-or-delete review. Pair with our library feature: bookmark here, process weekly.

## 5. Weekly Review (30 minutes)

Linked view of: completed tasks, open actions, calendar, goals. Five prompts: what shipped, what slipped, what did I learn, what's the one thing next week, what can I drop. The highest-ROI template on this list.

## 6. Habit Tracker (minimal)

Track max 3 habits. Binary checkboxes, weekly view. If a habit needs a 5-column tracker, the habit is too complicated — simplify the habit, not the template.

## 7. Content Pipeline

Ideas → Drafting → Editing → Published, with a publish-date calendar. Add an "AI assist" checkbox noting which pieces used AI drafts — transparency for future you (and sponsors).

## 8. Finance Snapshot

Monthly: income, fixed costs, variable spend, invested. One rollup: savings rate. You don't need a budgeting empire — you need to see the savings rate trend.

## 9. Project One-Pager

Every project gets: goal (1 sentence), non-goals, owner, deadline, status, next action. Forces the clarity conversation *before* the work starts.

## Rules for templates that stick

1. **Mandatory "why" fields** (why saved, why this project) filter junk at entry.
2. **Stale views** (untouched 7/30 days) create automatic review habits.
3. **Under 7 fields** per database. More fields = less usage.
4. **Weekly review** ties them together — templates without review rot.

## Key takeaways

- Copy systems, not aesthetics.
- Every database needs a "stale" review view.
- Fewer fields, more usage. Cap at three habits.
