# Best Valorant Settings for FPS and Aim (2026)

Your settings won't make you Radiant. But wrong settings *will* cap you — unstable FPS, muddy visuals, and a sensitivity that fights your muscle memory. Here's what competitive players actually use and how to find your own values.

## Graphics: clarity over beauty

Valorant is CPU-bound and designed to run on potatoes. Competitive settings minimize visual noise so enemies pop:

- **Display Mode:** Fullscreen (lowest input lag)
- **Resolution:** Native (1920×1080 for most). Stretched resolutions don't help in Valorant — unlike CS, the models don't widen usefully.
- **FPS Cap:** Uncapped, or cap 10% above your monitor's refresh rate if you get tearing without G-Sync.
- **Material Quality:** Low
- **Texture Quality:** Low–Medium (Medium if GPU headroom)
- **Detail Quality:** Low
- **Vignette:** Off. **VSync:** Off. **Anti-Aliasing:** MSAA 2x or FXAA.
- **Improve Clarity:** On. **Bloom:** Off. **First Person Shadows:** Off.

Expected result: 200+ FPS on a GTX 1660 / RTX 3050-class system, 400+ on modern mid-range.

## Sensitivity: find yours in 10 minutes

Most pros play **0.2–0.5 at 800 DPI** (160–400 eDPI). To find yours:

1. Set 800 DPI, 0.35 in-game.
2. Go to the Range, strafe and keep your crosshair glued to a bot's head.
3. If you overshoot consistently → lower by 0.02. Undershoot → raise by 0.02.
4. Confirm with 5 Deathmatches — your counter-strafe one-taps should feel *effortless*.

> One sensitivity for everything. Changing sens weekly destroys muscle memory. Give any new value 2 weeks before judging.

## Crosshair that disappears (in a good way)

A good crosshair is visible against every map texture and never covers heads at range:

```text
Color: Cyan (#00FFFF) | Outlines: On, 1px | Center Dot: Off
Inner Lines: 1/4/2/3 | Outer Lines: Off | Movement/Firing Error: Off
```

Turn off movement error once your strafing is decent — it adds visual noise without information.

## Audio + system

- **HRTF:** On (directional footsteps matter).
- **Windows:** Game Mode on, GPU scheduling on, mouse acceleration OFF (Enhance pointer precision unchecked), polling rate 1000Hz.
- **NVIDIA:** Low Latency Mode "Ultra" for GPU-bound setups; Reflex "On + Boost" in-game.

## Key takeaways

- Low settings for clarity, not just FPS.
- 160–400 eDPI range; adjust in 0.02 steps.
- One sensitivity, two weeks minimum before changing.
