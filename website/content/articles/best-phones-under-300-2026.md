# Best Phones Under ₹25,000 in 2026 (Tested Picks)

₹25,000 is the most competitive phone segment in India. After comparing cameras, displays, battery, and update policies, five phones stand out — for different buyers.

## How we rank

1. **Display** (AMOLED 120Hz minimum at this price)
2. **Main camera consistency** (daylight + low light, not megapixels)
3. **Battery + charging speed**
4. **Update policy** (2 OS updates minimum)
5. **After-sales network**

## Best Overall: Redmi Note 13 Pro (~₹24,000)

200MP main camera with OIS, curved 1.5K AMOLED, 67W charging, Snapdragon 7s Gen 2. The most complete package — camera, display, and design all punch above the price. MIUI needs 15 minutes of debloating (disable ads, uninstall bloat), but the hardware is unmatched here.

## Best Software: Moto G85 (~₹17,500)

If you hate bloatware, this is your phone: near-stock Android, useful Moto gestures, curved pOLED 144Hz display, stereo speakers. The Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 is a step below the Redmi for gaming, but daily use is smooth and the software experience is calmer.

## Best Camera for Photos: realme Narzo 70 Pro (~₹19,000)

The Sony IMX890 sensor with OIS — borrowed from ₹40K flagships — takes visibly cleaner night shots than rivals. If your priority is Instagram-ready low-light photos, nothing else under ₹20K competes.

## Best Battery Life: look for 6000mAh options

If you travel or face power cuts, a 6000mAh phone with 44W+ charging beats a better camera. Charge overnight anxiety disappears.

## Best Compact-ish: Pixel-class deals on sale

During Big Billion Days, the Pixel 8 has dropped near ₹35K — over budget, but worth knowing: sale prices rewrite every ranking. Never buy a week before a sale event.

## Buying tips

- **Storage:** 128GB is fine; pay extra for 256GB only if you shoot 4K video.
- **RAM:** 8GB is the floor in 2026.
- **Timing:** Flipkart/Amazon sales cut 10–20%. Set price alerts.

## Key takeaways

- Redmi Note 13 Pro for the complete package; Moto G85 for clean software; Narzo 70 Pro for night photos.
- Never buy right before a sale.
- 8GB RAM and AMOLED 120Hz are non-negotiable at ₹25K.
