# Fix "Storage Full" on Android Without Losing Data

The "Storage space running out" notification always arrives at the worst moment. Follow this exact order — it reclaims 5–15 GB in about 20 minutes, with zero data loss.

## Step 0: See what's actually full (2 min)

Go to **Settings → Storage**. Note the top 3 categories. For most people it's: WhatsApp media, gallery duplicates, and app cache. Knowing the breakdown stops you from deleting the wrong things.

## Step 1: Tame WhatsApp (biggest win, 5 min)

WhatsApp is the #1 storage hog on Indian phones — gigabytes of forwarded videos and "Good Morning" images.

1. Open WhatsApp → **Settings → Storage and data → Manage storage**.
2. Review **"Larger than 5 MB"** — delete forwarded videos you never watch.
3. Tap each heavy chat → select media → delete (keep the chat text).
4. Turn on **disappearing messages** (24h/7d) for noisy groups.
5. Disable auto-download: **Storage and data → Media auto-download →** uncheck everything on mobile data.

Typical recovery: **2–6 GB**.

## Step 2: Clear app cache — correctly (3 min)

**Settings → Apps →** sort by size. For heavy apps (Chrome, Instagram, YouTube), tap **Storage → Clear cache**. This deletes temporary files only — your logins and data stay.

> ⚠️ **Clear CACHE, not Clear DATA.** Clear Data logs you out and deletes app files. Cache is safe; Data is destructive.

Typical recovery: **1–3 GB**.

## Step 3: Offload photos and videos (5 min)

1. Open **Google Photos** → profile → **Back up** → confirm backup is complete.
2. Then tap **Free up space** — it removes only backed-up copies.
3. For videos, check **Files by Google → Clean → Large files** for screen recordings and downloads.

Typical recovery: **3–8 GB**.

## Step 4: Remove the silent hogs (5 min)

- **Files by Google → Clean**: delete memes, duplicates, old APKs, blurry photos.
- **Downloads folder**: old PDFs, installers, OTP screenshots.
- **Offline maps/music**: Google Maps offline areas and Spotify downloads pile up — remove ones you don't use.
- **Lite versions**: Facebook Lite, YouTube Go equivalents; disable preinstalled bloatware you never open (**Settings → Apps → Disable**).

## Step 5: Keep it from coming back

- Enable **Smart Storage** (Pixel) or auto-delete old media.
- Keep **15–20% free** — Android needs breathing room for updates and slows down when nearly full.
- Monthly 5-minute habit: WhatsApp Manage Storage + Files Clean suggestions.

| Action | Time | Typical recovery |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp media cleanup | 5 min | 2–6 GB |
| App cache clear | 3 min | 1–3 GB |
| Photos offload | 5 min | 3–8 GB |
| Downloads/duplicates | 5 min | 1–2 GB |

## Key takeaways

- Always clear **cache**, never **data**, unless you mean it.
- WhatsApp Manage Storage is the single biggest win.
- Keep 15–20% free so Android stays fast.
